Every video Build In Social creates now uses your cloned voice. Record a
60-second sample during onboarding, and every piece of content — across all
four platforms, in both manual and autopilot mode — sounds like you sat down
and recorded it.

someone reading a teleprompter. Build In Social adjusts cadence per platform:

- <Bold>60-second voice clone:</Bold> One recording powers all future content.

- <Bold>Platform-adapted pacing:</Bold> A 45-second LinkedIn video breathes
  differently than a 15-second X clip. The voice matches.
- <Bold>Works in both modes:</Bold> Whether you're sharing a specific story
  in manual mode or letting autopilot run your week, the voice is yours.

## Autopilot Mode

The feature that defines Build In Social. Set your domain during onboarding,
connect your platforms, and walk away. Every week, the system generates a full
batch of platform-native video content — 23 videos across YouTube Shorts,
Instagram Reels, LinkedIn, and X — without any input from you.

This is not random content. Autopilot draws from trending topics in your
niche, evergreen angles that perform for your audience type, and content
series designed to build authority over time. Each video is formatted for its
target platform: 30-45 seconds for YouTube Shorts, 20-30 seconds for
Instagram Reels, 45-60 seconds for LinkedIn, 15-20 seconds for X.

- <Bold>Full weekly batches:</Bold> ~23 videos per week across all connected
  platforms. Published on schedule, no manual steps.
- <Bold>Domain-aware content:</Bold> Videos are about your expertise — React
  patterns, SaaS metrics, infrastructure decisions — not generic filler.
- <Bold>pSEO pages:</Bold> Every published video generates a Google-indexed
  landing page with structured metadata and transcript. Your content works
  twice.

</ChangelogEntry>

sure the script has real substance. Autopilot mode for every other week,

- <Bold>Manual mode with quality gate:</Bold> Share a topic and answer three
  specific questions. No vague scripts, no generic advice videos.
- <Bold>14-day free trial:</Bold> Solo at $39/mo, Creator at $79/mo, Studio
  at $149/mo. Start with autopilot, add manual mode when you have something
